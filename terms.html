Feeding raw meat, organs, and raw edible bones carries inherent biological and mechanical risks, including exposure to foodborne pathogens (such as Salmonella, E. coli, and Listeria) and choking or gastrointestinal obstruction if inappropriate bones are fed. Users assume full responsibility for selecting appropriately sized raw edible bones, never feeding cooked bones, supervising their pets during feeding, and practicing strict kitchen sanitization.
