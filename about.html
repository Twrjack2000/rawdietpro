About RawDietPro.com

RawDietPro.com was created to eliminate the guesswork, dangerous calcium-to-phosphorus imbalances, and complicated kitchen math that pet owners face when formulating homemade Biologically Appropriate Raw Food (BARF) and Prey Model Raw (PMR) meals.

Our Nutritional Formulation Standards

Unlike generic "2.5% of body weight" calculators that treat a 6-pound Chihuahua identically to a 165-pound English Mastiff or a high-drive Belgian Malinois, our calculators account for:

  • Allometric & Breed-Specific Metabolic Scaling: Toy breeds expend significantly more calories per pound of body mass (3.2%–4.0% daily intake) than giant breeds (1.8%–2.2%), while working herding and tactical breeds require elevated fat and taurine/CoQ10 density.
  • Three Distinct Ratio Models: Instant switching between Dr. Ian Billinghurst's BARF Model (70% Muscle / 10% Bone / 10% Produce / 5% Liver / 5% Secreting Organ), strict Prey Model Raw (80/10/5/5), and high-calcium Puppy Growth (68/15/6/6/5).
  • Gram-Scale Precision & Micronutrient Closure: Every calculation outputs simultaneously in grams, ounces, and pounds (both daily and 30-day bulk freezer totals) while computing weight-scaled dosages for the four core micronutrients missing from supermarket meats (EPA/DHA Omega-3s, Kelp Iodine, Vitamin E, and Green-Lipped Mussel).
